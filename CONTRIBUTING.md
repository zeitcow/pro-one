# Contributing to Pro-One

Pro-One is an early-stage, free, open-source legal AI project.

Contributions should help the project become more useful, transparent, grounded, and accessible.

## Current status

The project is still defining its architecture and MVP. Early contributions should focus on documentation, architecture, corpus design, retrieval, evaluation, and small, reviewable improvements.

## Contribution principles

Contributions should:

- be narrow and reviewable
- avoid unsupported legal claims
- preserve source and citation information
- clearly distinguish planned features from implemented features
- avoid overstating what the project can do
- prioritize user safety and clarity
- respect that Pro-One provides legal information, not legal advice

## Legal information standard

Any future legal-information feature should be grounded in reliable sources.

Preferred sources include:

- statutes
- regulations
- court rules
- official forms
- official court self-help resources
- primary legal authorities

Secondary sources may be useful, but they should be clearly identified as secondary.

## AI usage

AI-generated code, documentation, or legal explanations should be carefully reviewed before being submitted.

Contributors are responsible for the content they submit.

AI should not be used to invent legal authority, citations, statutes, cases, or source text.

## Pull request expectations

A pull request should explain:

- what changed
- why the change is useful
- whether the change affects legal information, retrieval, citations, or user-facing output
- how the change was reviewed or tested

## Legal disclaimer

Pro-One is not a lawyer and does not provide legal advice.

Contributions should not create the impression that Pro-One can replace a qualified attorney or provide case-specific legal advice.

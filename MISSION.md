# Mission

Pro-One exists to make practical legal help more accessible to people and small businesses handling legal matters on their own.

The repository is currently an architecture and specification foundation. It does not yet contain a production legal AI application or publicly supported legal workflow.

The project is based on a simple idea: legal information should be easier to understand, easier to use, and easier to verify.

Pro-One is intended to be free, open-source legal AI that helps users understand legal processes, organize facts and documents, and complete common legal tasks through structured workflows grounded in reliable legal sources.

## Core mission

Pro-One's mission is to expand access to legal information by building free and open tools that help users:

- understand legal processes in plain language
- organize facts, documents, deadlines, and questions
- follow structured legal workflows
- find and understand relevant legal sources
- prepare user-confirmed draft structures or checklists that users can review before taking action
- know when the system cannot safely or reliably answer

## Who Pro-One is for

Pro-One is being designed for:

- self-represented litigants
- individuals dealing with common civil legal problems
- small businesses handling basic legal disputes or documents
- people who need help understanding legal processes before deciding what to do next

The project should prioritize users who may not have easy access to legal help.

## What Pro-One should be

Pro-One should be:

- **free:** core access should not depend on a user's ability to pay
- **open source:** the software should be inspectable, reusable, and open to contribution
- **grounded:** legal information should be supported by reliable sources
- **practical:** tools should focus on real legal tasks and workflows
- **transparent:** users should see sources, limitations, and uncertainty
- **accessible:** explanations should be clear, usable, and written for non-lawyers
- **human-directed:** users should remain in control of legal decisions

## What Pro-One should not be

Pro-One should not be:

- an AI lawyer
- a substitute for a qualified attorney
- a source of unsupported legal advice
- a system that invents legal authority
- a tool that hides uncertainty from users
- a paywalled legal advice product
- a system that pressures users into legal decisions

## Long-term vision

The long-term vision is to build a reliable legal workflow assistant for common civil legal matters.

Possible future workflows may include:

- understanding and responding to court papers
- preparing basic litigation responses
- tracking litigation steps and deadlines
- organizing contract disputes
- preparing demand letters
- reviewing common contract terms
- helping small businesses organize legal issues and documents

Each workflow should be narrow, source-grounded, and designed around real user needs.

## Guiding principle

Pro-One should expand legal access without pretending that AI can replace legal judgment.

The project should help users understand, organize, and verify legal information while making clear where professional legal help may be needed.
